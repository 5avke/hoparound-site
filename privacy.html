PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: October 23, 2026

This policy explains what information the HOP-AROUND! mobile game ("the game", "we") collects, why, and your choices.

What we collect

  • Account information: when you sign in with Apple we receive an account identifier and an email address (this may be a private relay address from Apple).
  • Player name: the name you choose, shown to other players.
  • Game progress: trophies, items, currencies, skins and settings, saved so you keep your progress across devices.
  • Purchase history: which in-app items you bought, used to deliver and restore them.
  • Player reports: if you report a player, we store the report (who reported, who was reported, and the reason) to keep the game safe.

What we do not collect

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, or payment card details (payments are handled by Apple). Voice and text chat between friends goes directly between devices and is not recorded or stored by us. We do not sell your data and we do not use it for advertising tracking.

How we use it

Only to run the game: your account and cloud save, online matches and rankings, delivering purchases, and handling reports and support requests.

Who processes it

  • Supabase: account sign-in, cloud saves, player names and reports.
  • RevenueCat: in-app purchase records.
  • Apple: Sign in with Apple and payment processing.
  • PeerJS signaling server: briefly connects two players for an online match; it does not store game data.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your data while your account exists. You can delete your account at any time in the game: Settings → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account, player name and saved progress. Purchase records may be kept by Apple and RevenueCat as required for tax and accounting.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
